# Large-Scale 3D Concrete Printer (3DCP): Prototype Design

A gantry-type 3D concrete printer with a **1.5 m × 1 m × 1 m** build volume, designed during my B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering (Apr 2024 onwards).

## My role
- Led the mechanical design of the printer.
- Designed the **extrusion system**: hopper, nozzle and drive.
- Coordinated vendors for the motion system, electronics and materials, and completed the bill of materials.

## Files
| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `cad/final-assembly-v2.iges` | Full printer assembly (neutral format; opens in SolidWorks, Fusion 360, FreeCAD, etc.) |
| `cad/frame.iges` | Gantry frame |
| `cad/assembly-v21.f3z` | Native Fusion 360 assembly archive |
| `simulation/3dcp.wbpj` + `3dcp_files/` | ANSYS Workbench project. Open the `.wbpj` file with the `3dcp_files` folder next to it. |

## Tools
SolidWorks · Fusion 360 · ANSYS Workbench
